Private by design

Okanzo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Okanzo is an offline-first net worth tracker published by Cairn Labworks. It is built so that your financial data stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data, we have no user accounts, and we do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What stays on your device

All of your portfolios, assets, liabilities, notes and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted SQLCipher database (AES-256). The database key is held in the Android Keystore. Your data never leaves the device except when you deliberately export an encrypted, password-protected backup file, which you control.

Network access

Okanzo makes network requests only to look up public market data. These requests contain the minimum needed and never include your portfolio values, item names, balances, or any personal information:

  • Market prices — when you refresh or tap fetch-price on a market-linked asset, only the ticker symbol is sent to the public Yahoo Finance endpoint (query1.finance.yahoo.com).
  • Currency exchange rates — refreshed automatically (on launch and at most once every 12 hours) so totals display correctly. Only a base currency code is sent to open.er-api.com.

These are third-party services with their own privacy practices; Okanzo sends them no identifying data. Because they are non-free network services, this is disclosed on the app's F-Droid listing as an anti-feature.

Data we collect

None. Okanzo has no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party trackers. We cannot see your data because it never reaches us.

Permissions

Okanzo requests only internet access (for the market lookups above) and, optionally, biometric/device-credential access if you enable the app lock.

Children's privacy

Okanzo is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children.
